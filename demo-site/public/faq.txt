## What is Acme?
Acme is a project management tool that supports up to 500 projects per account. It lets teams organize tasks, track deadlines, and collaborate in real time.

## How do I create a new project?
Click the "+ New project" button on the top-right of the Dashboard page. A modal will open — enter a project name, choose a type (Software, Design, Marketing, or Research) and a priority (Low, Medium, High), then click "Create project".

## How do I invite team members?
Click "Team" in the left sidebar. Then click the "+ Invite member" button in the top-right. Enter the email address, choose a role (Admin or Member), and click "Send invite".

## How do I upgrade my plan?
Click "Billing" in the left sidebar. Choose a plan (Pro at $29/mo or Enterprise at $99/mo) and click the Confirm button at the bottom. You can also reach Billing via Settings — click "Upgrade plan" in the Subscription section.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Acme does not currently offer self-serve cancellation. Contact support@acme.com to cancel your subscription.

## How many projects can I have?
The Pro plan supports unlimited projects. The Free plan is limited to 3 active projects.

## How do I delete a project?
Open the project from the Dashboard and click the three-dot menu, then select "Delete Project". This action cannot be undone.

## How do I change my account settings?
Click "Settings" in the left sidebar. You can update your full name, email address, and notification preferences (email and Slack). Click "Save changes" when done.

## How do I change my password?
Go to Settings in the left sidebar. Scroll to the Security section and click "Change Password".

## Is there a mobile app?
Acme is available as a web app and a mobile app for iOS and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.

## How do I contact support?
Email support@acme.com. The support team responds within 24 hours on business days.

## What pages does Acme have?
The left sidebar has four pages: Dashboard (overview with stats and recent projects), Settings (profile and notifications), Billing (plan selection and invoices), and Team (member list and invites).
